# Kairos Reflection Journal

A self-contained browser-based journaling and reflection prototype. Open pattern_reflection_journal.html directly in a browser.

## What it does

The app applies the project's ten-stage vocabulary to text or user-entered check-ins using deterministic word/letter digit-root arithmetic. It can save readings and track the user's own repeated stage selections over time.

The arithmetic is a transparent pattern-generation rule. It does not measure meaning, personality, mental health, nervous-system state, or objective truth. A random string can produce a stage just as a meaningful journal entry can.

## Micro-position

The optional micro-position repeats the same digit-root operation on a smaller text segment. The UI uses generic labels for intermediate decimals. This is a reflection convention, not a precise or validated instrument.

The broader 81-stage structure is a framework concept. This app does not provide a complete, independently validated set of 81 distinct micro-stage definitions.

## Additional calculations

The app includes project-defined stage parity, Stage 5 prompts, Stage 8 repeat-count flags, and Shannon entropy over a user's own check-in history. These are deterministic calculations on the supplied inputs. They do not establish therapeutic benefit, risk, clinical state, or future outcomes.

## Journals

1. Pattern Scan — reflect a text or topic through the stage vocabulary.
2. Cycle Journal — track stage labels across self-selected life areas and count repeated entries.
3. Habit Log — use a simple checklist of reflective habits.

## AI-enabled Kairos app

kairos_app.html optionally connects to the Kairos backend for AI-generated narratives and follow-up questions. If enabled, the submitted text is sent to the configured backend and Anthropic's Claude API. AI-generated story and quote parallels are not source-verified; independently verify any attribution.

## Data handling

The base journal stores its entries in browser local storage. The AI layer is optional and sends the text you choose to submit to the backend and Anthropic. Do not submit highly sensitive information unless you have reviewed the data-handling arrangements.

## Limitations

Kairos is a reflection and journaling prototype, not therapy, diagnosis, treatment, a crisis assessment, or a substitute for professional care. The framework's physical/consciousness stability labels are project-defined indices, not physiological measurements.
